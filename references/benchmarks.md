# Inspiration benchmarks (principles, not prescriptions)

These are useful reputational references, **not** claims that any specific current screen is award-winning or optimal. Do not reproduce their layouts, copy, assets or trademarks. Verify any current award or performance claim independently before citing.

- **Chewy:** empathy, reassurance, repeat purchasing and service recovery.
- **Apple:** guided hardware selection, comparison, configuration and confident purchase.
- **Nike:** emotional storytelling, membership value and connected brand ecosystem.
- **Sephora:** discovery, personalization, education and loyalty.
- **Airbnb:** discovery, availability, booking and confirmation.
- **Oura / Eight Sleep:** explain hardware-plus-recurring-service value and ongoing customer relationship.
- **Stripe:** precision in complex forms, billing, trust and account self-service.
- **Rivian / Tesla:** guided consideration and consultation/test-drive pathways for expensive products.
- **BASIC/DEPT, Build in Amsterdam, Work & Co:** creative direction, high-craft commerce and product experience case studies.

## Applying references responsibly
1. Identify a *principle* relevant to the user journey (e.g. confidence through progressive configuration).
2. Map it to the actual customer problem and brand.
3. Fit it to current platform and accessibility constraints.
4. Validate with customers and measurement; never use reputation as proof of conversion uplift.
