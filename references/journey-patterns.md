# Journey patterns

Select only patterns relevant to the current customer job. Avoid one-size-fits-all funnels.

## Ecommerce and product discovery
- Discovery: task-based navigation, understandable categories, helpful search, useful filters, clear product differences and zero-results recovery.
- PDP: value proposition, clear images and demonstrations, compatibility/fit, availability, total cost, shipping/returns, reviews, support and prominent purchase action.
- Configuration: show steps, selections, constraints, price impact and ability to edit without losing progress.
- Cart: preserve selections; show fulfillment, discounts, subscriptions and total cost honestly; avoid unexpected detours.
- After purchase: confirmation, tracking, setup, registration, support and easy repeat purchase.
- Measure: findability, product engagement, add-to-cart, checkout progression, conversion, returns and support contacts; segment by device and customer type.

## Booking and appointments
- Show what is being booked, location/service area, duration, prerequisites, availability, price or whether a quote is required.
- Use calendar controls with clear timezone, locale, unavailable dates, accessible keyboard interaction and visible slot confirmation.
- Keep chosen slot while collecting only essential details; provide change/cancel policies before commitment.
- Handle slot contention, expired availability, network failure and rescheduling without silent loss.
- Confirm with clear date/time, location or meeting method, preparation and next steps.
- Measure: booking start-to-complete, abandonment by step, reschedule rate, no-show rate, support burden.

## Memberships and subscriptions
- Distinguish one-time hardware purchase, recurring membership, free trial, included service and optional add-ons.
- Disclose billing frequency, first charge date, trial end, renewal amount, tax treatment if known, commitment, cancellation and downgrade implications.
- Make plan comparison legible and accessible; explain entitlements and what happens on upgrade/downgrade.
- In account UI, show current plan, next bill, payment method, invoices, pause/cancel/change paths and resulting effective dates.
- For hardware-linked service, cover activation, device pairing, eligibility, coverage, offline states and subscription-required functionality.
- Never imply Shopify natively handles arbitrary membership entitlements; verify installed app, billing provider and account architecture.
- Measure: trial activation, trial-to-paid, churn, self-service success, involuntary churn, contact rate.

## Lead generation and guided selling
- Start with the customer's question/problem rather than internal sales qualification.
- Explain the value of submitting information and what happens next, including expected contact method/timing only if verified.
- Ask minimal fields initially; progressive qualification only when it materially improves the recommendation or routing.
- For property/service solutions, consider location, product suitability, installation complexity, estimated next steps and appointment scheduling.
- Provide privacy/consent clarity; separate marketing opt-in from necessary service contact.
- Confirm submission, provide next steps and an alternative contact path; support retries and prevent duplicates.
- Measure: qualified lead rate, form completion, appointment booked, show rate, lead-to-sale, customer effort.

## Cross-journey edge cases
Localization, currency, taxes, consent, network failure, out-of-stock/unavailable, authentication expiry, accessibility, small screens, returning customers, payment/booking errors, and handoff to external systems.
